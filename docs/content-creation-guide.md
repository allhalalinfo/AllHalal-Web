# Content Creation Guide - AllHalal.info

## 📝 For Content Writers & Editors

Этот гайд поможет создавать качественный контент для AllHalal.info, соответствующий нашим editorial standards и SEO требованиям.

---

## 🎯 Content Philosophy

### Наши принципы:
1. **Authority & Trust** - Мы источник правды о halal lifestyle
2. **Practical Value** - Каждая статья должна помогать читателю
3. **Cultural Sensitivity** - Уважение к различным madhabs и мнениям
4. **Transparency** - Указываем источники, методологию, обновления
5. **User-First** - Пишем для людей, не для поисковых систем

---

## 📋 Content Types

### 1. Blog Posts (Новости, обзоры, короткие статьи)
**Где**: `/blog`  
**Длина**: 1200-2000 слов  
**Обновления**: Не требуются (если не seasonal)  
**Примеры**:
- "Top 10 Halal Restaurants Opening in 2026"
- "Interview with Chef Ahmad: Michelin-Starred Halal Cuisine"
- "Ramadan 2026: Dates & Preparation Guide"

### 2. Evergreen Guides (Длинные, обновляемые гайды)
**Где**: `/guides`  
**Длина**: 2500-4000 слов  
**Обновления**: Каждые 6 месяцев  
**Обязательно**: TOC, Sources, Last Updated, Version  
**Примеры**:
- "Complete Guide to Halal Certification"
- "Islamic Finance 101: Everything You Need to Know"
- "Dubai Travel Guide for Muslims (2026 Edition)"

### 3. Listicles (Топ-N, подборки)
**Где**: `/blog`, category pages  
**Длина**: 1200-2000 слов  
**Структура**: Intro + N items (каждый 150-300 слов) + Conclusion  
**Примеры**:
- "15 Best Halal Fine Dining Restaurants in London"
- "Top 10 Muslim-Friendly Cities to Visit"

### 4. Explainers (Объяснения терминов, концепций)
**Где**: `/guides`, topic pages  
**Длина**: 1500-2500 слов  
**Структура**: Definition → How it works → Examples → FAQ  
**Примеры**:
- "What Is Murabaha Financing?"
- "Muslim-Owned vs. Halal-Certified: Key Differences"

### 5. Programmatic Pages (Auto-generated база)
**Где**: `/restaurants/[city]`, `/travel/country/[country]`, etc.  
**Длина**: 800-1200 слов  
**Обязательно**: Unique intro, Quick facts, FAQ, Internal links  
**Примеры**:
- "Halal Restaurants in Paris"
- "Downtown Dubai: Real Estate Guide"

## ✍️ Writing Standards

### Title (H1)
- **Length**: 50-60 characters
- **Format**: Descriptive + Benefit + Year (if relevant)
- **Good**: "Best Halal Fine Dining in London (2026 Guide)"
- **Bad**: "Restaurants" (too vague)

### Meta Description
- **Length**: 150-160 characters
- **Include**: Primary keyword + value proposition + CTA
- **Good**: "Discover 15 Michelin-starred halal restaurants in London, from fine dining to family-friendly spots. Complete with addresses, menus & reviews."
- **Bad**: "This article is about halal restaurants." (not compelling)

### Introduction (First 100 words)
- Answer the main question immediately
- Include primary keyword naturally
- Promise what reader will learn
- Hook with statistic, question, or bold statement

**Good Intro Example:**
> Dubai is home to over 2,500 halal-certified restaurants, including 8 Michelin-starred establishments. Whether you're looking for luxury fine dining or authentic street food, this complete guide covers the 30 best halal restaurants in Dubai across all cuisines and budgets. Last updated: January 2026.

### Body Structure
- Use H2/H3 subheadings every 300-500 words
- Keep paragraphs 2-4 sentences max
- Use bullet points for lists
- Add callout boxes for important info
- Include images/maps where relevant

### Internal Linking
**Minimum**: 3-5 links per article  
**Where to link**:
- Related articles in same category
- Evergreen guides
- Tools (ingredient checker, travel checklist)
- Hub pages (main category pages)
- Programmatic pages (cities, countries, areas)

**Example in-text link:**
> "All restaurants listed are halal-certified by recognized [certification bodies](/certification/bodies) such as HMC and HFA."

### External Links (Sources)
- Link to authoritative sources (JAKIM, IFANCA, scholarly articles)
- Use `rel="nofollow"` for commercial links
- Always open in new tab (`target="_blank"`)
- Cite in "Sources" section at end

## 📊 Content Components (Must-Have)

### 1. Table of Contents (TOC)
**Required for**: Guides 2000+ words  
**Format**: Auto-generated from H2/H3 headings  
**Example**:
```
Contents:
1. What Is Halal Certification?
2. Major Certification Bodies Worldwide
3. How to Verify Certification
4. FAQs
```

### 2. Quick Facts Box
**Required for**: Location pages, developer/bank profiles  
**Example**:
```
Quick Facts: Downtown Dubai
📍 Location: Central Dubai
🏢 Developers: 8+
🏠 Property Types: Apartments, Penthouses
💰 Price Range: AED 2,500 - 5,000 per sq ft
🕌 Mosques Nearby: 5
🍽️ Halal Restaurants: 120+
```

### 3. FAQ Section
**Required for**: ALL articles (minimum 3 questions)  
**Format**: Question → Clear answer (50-150 words)  
**SEO**: Enables FAQPage structured data  
**Example**:
```
## Frequently Asked Questions

### Is gelatin always haram?
No. Gelatin can be halal if derived from halal-slaughtered animals or fish. 
Always check for halal certification on products containing gelatin.

### What are halal alternatives to gelatin?
Agar-agar (from seaweed), pectin (from fruit), and carrageenan are plant-based
alternatives commonly used in halal products.
```

### 4. Sources Section
**Required for**: Guides, explainers, certification topics  
**Format**: Numbered list with clickable links  
**Example**:
```
## Sources
1. [JAKIM Halal Standards](https://www.halal.gov.my) - Malaysian Halal Authority
2. [IFANCA Guidelines](https://www.ifanca.org) - Islamic Food and Nutrition Council
3. Dr. Ahmad, Islamic Finance Expert - Personal Interview (Jan 2026)
```

### 5. Last Updated / Version
**Required for**: Evergreen guides  
**Format**: Prominent date stamp + version number  
**Example**:
```
**Last Updated**: January 14, 2026
**Version**: 2.1
**Last Reviewed By**: [Author Name], Halal Certification Expert
```

### 6. Author Bio
**Required for**: All articles  
**Format**: Short bio (2-3 sentences) + expertise + photo  
**Example**:
```
**About the Author**
Sarah Ahmad is a halal food consultant with 10+ years of experience in 
Islamic finance and certification. She has advised restaurants across the 
Middle East and Europe on halal compliance.
```

### 7. Related Content
**Required for**: All articles (end of page)  
**Format**: 3-6 related links (mix of similar articles + guides + tools)  
**Example**:
```
## Continue Your Journey
- [Best Halal Restaurants in London](/restaurants/london)
- [What Is Halal Certification?](/guides/halal-certification)
- [Ingredient Checker Tool](/tools/ingredient-checker)
```

## 🎨 Callout Boxes

Use callout boxes to highlight important information:

### 💡 Tip
```
💡 **Pro Tip**: Always ask the restaurant manager to show their halal 
certificate. Legitimate halal restaurants will have it readily available.
```

### ⚠️ Warning
```
⚠️ **Important**: Some E-codes have different halal statuses depending on 
the source. Always check with the manufacturer or look for halal certification.
```

### ℹ️ Note
```
ℹ️ **Note**: Madhabs may have different opinions on certain ingredients. 
This guide reflects the majority opinion. Consult your local scholar for 
specific guidance.
```

### 📌 Disclosure
```
📌 **Disclosure**: Some links in this article are affiliate links. We may 
earn a commission if you make a purchase, at no additional cost to you.
```

## 🔍 SEO Best Practices

### Keywords
- **Primary keyword**: In H1, first 100 words, 1-2 H2s, URL
- **Secondary keywords**: Naturally throughout content
- **Keyword density**: 1-2% (don't force it)
- **LSI keywords**: Use variations and related terms

### URLs
- **Format**: `/category/descriptive-slug`
- **Good**: `/restaurants/dubai/best-halal-fine-dining`
- **Bad**: `/page123`, `/restaurants/dubai/article-new-v2`

### Images
- Always use `next/image` for optimization
- **Alt text**: Descriptive + keyword (naturally)
- **File names**: `halal-restaurant-dubai-marina.jpg`
- **Size**: Compress before upload (max 500KB)
- **Format**: WebP preferred, JPEG fallback

### Internal Linking Opportunities
Always link when mentioning:
- Other cities → `/restaurants/[city]` or `/travel/cities/[city]`
- Countries → `/travel/country/[country]`
- Ingredients → `/ingredients/[slug]`
- E-codes → `/e-codes/[code]`
- Certifiers → `/certification/bodies/[slug]`
- Developers → `/real-estate/developers/[slug]`
- Areas → `/real-estate/dubai/[area]`

## 📅 Update Schedule

### Evergreen Guides
- Review every **6 months**
- Update "Last Updated" date
- Increment version number (e.g., 2.1 → 2.2)
- Check all external links still work
- Update statistics if available

### Seasonal Content
- **Ramadan**: Update 1-2 months before
- **Hajj/Umrah**: Update 2-3 months before
- **Eid**: Update 1 month before
- **Travel seasons**: Update quarterly

### Programmatic Pages
- Update when data changes (new restaurants, developers, areas)
- Check facts quarterly
- Refresh FAQ if new questions arise

### News/Blog Posts
- No regular updates needed
- If event-based, mark as "archived" after event

## ✅ Pre-Publish Checklist

Before submitting any article, check:

### Content
- [ ] Title is 50-60 characters
- [ ] Meta description is 150-160 characters
- [ ] Introduction answers main question in first 100 words
- [ ] Subheadings every 300-500 words
- [ ] Paragraphs are 2-4 sentences max
- [ ] 3-5 internal links included
- [ ] FAQ section (minimum 3 questions)
- [ ] Sources cited (if applicable)
- [ ] Images have alt text
- [ ] Author bio included
- [ ] Related content links (3-6 items)

### SEO
- [ ] Primary keyword in H1, intro, 1-2 H2s
- [ ] URL is descriptive
- [ ] Images compressed & optimized
- [ ] All external links open in new tab
- [ ] Internal links go to correct pages

### Quality
- [ ] Spell-checked & grammar-checked
- [ ] Facts verified
- [ ] No plagiarism (original content)
- [ ] Culturally sensitive language
- [ ] Accessible language (avoid jargon or explain it)

### Editorial Policy Compliance
- [ ] Methodology explained (for comparisons/rankings)
- [ ] Disclaimers included (finance/real estate)
- [ ] Affiliate links disclosed
- [ ] No biased recommendations without disclosure

## 🚫 What NOT to Do

### Don't:
❌ Copy-paste from other websites (plagiarism)  
❌ Use AI-generated content without heavy editing  
❌ Make fatwa/religious rulings (cite scholars instead)  
❌ Recommend specific financial products without disclaimer  
❌ Write click-bait titles that don't deliver  
❌ Stuff keywords unnaturally  
❌ Use offensive/insensitive language  
❌ Promise "guaranteed halal" without certification proof  
❌ Link to questionable sources  
❌ Forget to update "Last Updated" date on guides

## 💬 Tone & Voice

### AllHalal Voice
- **Authoritative but approachable** - Expert, not academic
- **Helpful and practical** - Focus on "how to" not just "what is"
- **Respectful and inclusive** - Acknowledge different madhabs/opinions
- **Confident but humble** - "We recommend" not "This is the only way"
- **Clear and concise** - No fluff, get to the point

### Example Sentences
✅ **Good**: "Most scholars agree that gelatin from halal-slaughtered cattle is permissible. However, Hanafi scholars may differ. Consult your local imam for guidance."

❌ **Bad**: "Gelatin is halal. Period."

✅ **Good**: "Based on our evaluation of 50+ halal restaurants in London, here are our top 15 picks for 2026."

❌ **Bad**: "These are the best restaurants ever and you must visit them."

## 🏆 Content Quality Tiers

### Tier 1: Excellent ⭐⭐⭐
- Comprehensive, original research
- 10+ internal links
- 5+ authoritative sources
- Rich media (images, charts, maps)
- Updated regularly
- High engagement (comments, shares)

### Tier 2: Good ⭐⭐
- Solid content, good research
- 5+ internal links
- 3+ sources
- Images included
- Meets all checklist items

### Tier 3: Acceptable ⭐
- Meets minimum standards
- 3 internal links
- Basic FAQ
- At least 1 image
- Passable but room for improvement

**Goal**: Every article should be Tier 2 minimum, strive for Tier 1.
